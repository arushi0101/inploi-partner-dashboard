The reasoning behind those actions

Partners delivered the largest new logo of the year

Six new logos were booked in 2026 for £273,030. Mitchells & Butlers is £87,000 of that — the single biggest new customer deal, and 31.9% of all net new revenue, from one partner. Only Compass Group's International Hub booked more, and that came through an account inploi already had. Partners are not the whole of new business, but they produced the biggest new relationship in it from a standing start.

Report partner revenue off Closed Won Date

Won deals move into the Implementation pipeline, so their stage stops being a closed-won stage. A stage filter would miss this win and every one after it. One filter choice, made once, and partner revenue reports correctly from here on.

Enablement is the lever, not recruitment

Six partners are signed and three have never sourced a deal, while two producers — Arctic Shores and ModusForce — have no agreement at all. Those relationships already exist and are warm, so Eploy, Pinpoint and HospitalityJobs are the shortest path to more producing partners — a cheaper move than finding anyone new.

Sourcing is compounding since the CRM went in

Eleven deals in eight weeks from four different partners, starting the week the partner records were built. It is not one relationship carrying the number, which means the pattern should hold.

Beamery is the engine — protect it

Six of this year's fifteen deals and £69,100 of open pipeline. Its NDA lapsed on 14 June, so renewing it is the single highest-value piece of admin on this page. Worth pairing with a conversation about what a second Beamery-shaped relationship would take.

Capacity is the main lever available

One manager, fifty-nine relationships, five producing partners and £87,000 won — just under a third of everything new booked this year. That gives a concrete basis for the resourcing conversation: what one person produced, and what the next would be expected to add.
